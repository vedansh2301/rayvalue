summary: "CNN reported that Hurricane Milton knocked out power for more than 2.5 million customers across Florida in October 2024, including in Manatee County, but homes in Hunters Point, a new development between Sarasota Bay and Tampa Bay, kept their lights on. Residents who stayed through Helene and Milton reported little to no damage, and solar power and battery storage kept the lights on all night and the following days. The homes were designed to withstand Category 5 hurricanes, with living space 16 feet above sea level, and were selling for $1.4 million to $1.9 million; 31 of the 86 planned homes were complete."
lesson: "The batteries are what kept these homes running, and they came with unusually storm-hardened, expensive construction; if backup power is your goal, price the battery, not just the panels."
