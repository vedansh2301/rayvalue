summary: "pv magazine reported that Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory data show 74% of new residential solar installations in California included storage in 2025, up from 58% in 2024, while the share across the U.S. rose to 37% from 25%. The article ties California's shift to the net billing tariff in effect since April 2023, which cut the value of exported rooftop power in many hours and made storing daytime solar for later use more valuable. The median home battery was 13.5 kWh in both years."
lesson: "In California a battery is now the usual part of a new solar quote, so compare solar alone with solar plus a battery for your own usage before you decide."
