date_reported: 2022-04-28
summary: "Florida's HB 741 would have cut what utilities pay rooftop solar owners for exported power from 11 cents to 2 cents per kilowatt-hour by 2029 for new customers, and Gov. Ron DeSantis vetoed it in April 2022. Canary Media reported the bill would not have applied to the roughly 100,000 existing net-metered customers, and that opponents of the bill pointed out that less than 1 percent of the state's 10.5 million electric customers have net-metered solar. The bill was largely drafted by lobbyists for Florida Power & Light, according to a Miami Herald and Floodlight investigation."
lesson: "Export rules can change after you install, so check how your utility credits exported power and don't assume today's terms are permanent."
