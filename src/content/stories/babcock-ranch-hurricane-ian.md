summary: "Babcock Ranch, a neighborhood of roughly 2,000 homes about 12 miles northeast of Fort Myers, kept power during Hurricane Ian in 2022 while the storm knocked out power to more than 2.6 million Florida customers. CNN reported that a nearby array of 700,000 solar panels produces more electricity than the neighborhood uses, and that some residents had added rooftop panels and batteries. The storm uprooted trees and tore shingles from roofs, but a resident described no other major damage."
lesson: "One neighborhood's experience shows what is possible, not what a single home with panels will get; batteries are what let a house run during an outage, and they add cost."
