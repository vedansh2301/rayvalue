summary: "The News Service of Florida reported that the Florida Public Service Commission approved a four-year rate settlement for Florida Power & Light that raises base rates by $945 million in 2026 and $705 million in 2027, with more collected in 2028 and 2029 for solar and battery-storage projects. In FPL's traditional territory, a 1,000 kWh residential bill goes from $134.14 to $136.64 in 2026, while Northwest Florida customers see a drop from $143.60 to $141.36, with increases projected in later years. The Office of Public Counsel and consumer groups said the deal could total about $6.9 billion over four years and expected to challenge it at the Florida Supreme Court."
lesson: "FPL's rates are now set for several years but still projected to rise, so run your payback with your own bill and a range of rate growth, not a single forecast."
