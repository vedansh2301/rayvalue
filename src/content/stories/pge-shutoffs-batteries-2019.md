summary: "ABC7 reported that during PG&E's public safety power shutoffs, solar panels without storage shut off with the grid as a safety precaution, and local installers saw higher interest in batteries. Sunrun said several hundred of its homes kept lights, TVs and refrigerators running through the events. One San Jose homeowner with 31 roof panels and a battery installed about 18 months earlier stayed powered through a shutoff and reported about 24 hours off the grid."
