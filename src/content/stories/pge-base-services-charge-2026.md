state: CA
location: "PG&E territory"
date_reported: 2026-03-01
summary: "PG&E's rate advisory says its average residential non-CARE rate fell from 41.46 to 40.60 cents per kWh on March 1, 2026, lowering the typical 500 kWh monthly bill from $208.68 to $203.54, or 2.5%. PG&E named two main drivers: the end of $499.5 million in wildfire cost recovery, and the start of the Base Services Charge, which reduces per-kWh rates for all residential customers. The typical CARE bill fell 8.11%, to $117.42."
lesson: "A lower price per kWh means each kWh your panels make saves a little less, and a fixed monthly charge stays on the bill either way, so estimate payback with current rates, not last year's."
