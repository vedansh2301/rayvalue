title: "After California cut export credits, rooftop solar installs fell and batteries became the norm"
state: CA
location: "California (PG&E, SCE and SDG&E territories)"
date_reported: 2024-05-23
summary: "Canary Media reported that California's net billing tariff, which replaced net metering on April 15, 2023, cut the value of solar sent to the grid by about 75 percent on average. Installs averaged about 8,000 per month in Q1 2024, lower than any month under net metering since May 2020, and CALSSA reported monthly sales down 77 to 85 percent for May through September 2023 versus 2022. About 60 percent of net-billing systems included batteries, versus about 10 percent under net metering, and inflation-adjusted prices for paired solar-battery systems rose about 17 percent."
lesson: "Under net billing, solar alone earns far less on exports than it used to, so expect quotes with a battery, and test whether the added cost pays back for your home."
