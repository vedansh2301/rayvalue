summary: "Florida Phoenix reported that the federal law signed on July 4, 2025 ends the 30% tax break for individuals buying rooftop solar by the end of 2025, and that installers were busy as a result. Tampa Bay Solar said it had about 100 new customers in the previous month, after not reaching 100 customers in the first four months of the year. The article also noted that Florida's investor-owned utilities installed the second-largest amount of solar capacity in the country in 2024, behind Texas, and that solar supplies 8% of the state's energy portfolio."
lesson: "That tax break is gone for systems installed in 2026 and later, so older quotes and payback figures that counted it no longer apply; compare current prices without it."
