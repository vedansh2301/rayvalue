title: "Berkeley Lab: solar plus a battery can cover essentials for days, but not always cooling"
state: FL
location: "Florida and the United States"
date_reported: 2022-09-29
summary: "As Hurricane Ian approached Florida, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory researchers reported that a modest solar system with one battery can power critical loads such as refrigeration, lighting, internet and well pumps for days at a time almost anywhere in the U.S. In seven of the 10 past outages they modeled, most homes could also keep heating and cooling running with solar and 30 kWh of storage, roughly two Powerwalls. Cloudy weather after a hurricane can limit solar output, and they put the installed cost of a Powerwall system at $12,000 to $16,500 before incentives and taxes."
lesson: "A battery can keep the essentials running in a multi-day outage, but whole-home air conditioning is the hard part and the system costs thousands of dollars more than solar alone."
