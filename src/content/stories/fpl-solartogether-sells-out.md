date_reported: 2021-07-26
summary: "pv magazine USA reported that residential subscriptions to FPL's SolarTogether program sold out, with more than 48,000 confirmed subscribers and about 2,000 more on a waiting list. Subscribers pay for a share of the program and receive bill credits rather than power from a specific project, and they were expected to reach a simple payback within seven years. The article also noted that the program's 74.5 MW projects are much larger than typical 1 to 5 MW community solar, so calling it community solar may be a misnomer."
lesson: "A subscription can lower bills without panels on your roof, but payback depends on credits that vary, and you get no backup power in an outage."
