date_reported: 2024-12-20
summary: "The News Service of Florida reported that state regulators approved a $184.9 million base-rate increase for Tampa Electric starting in January 2025, with further increases of $86.6 million in 2026 and $9.1 million in 2027. A residential customer using 1,000 kWh a month was projected to see the bill rise from $136.44 to $145.58. Regulators trimmed the utility's original 2025 request of $287.9 million and approved a 10.5% return on equity, between the 11.5% Tampa Electric sought and the 9.5% consumer advocates argued for."
lesson: "Florida rates move in steps through rate cases, so test a solar payback against a range of future prices instead of assuming one fixed yearly increase."
