## The short answer

Quotes are only comparable when they describe the same system. Ask every installer for the same size, equipment and terms, then compare them line by line instead of by the headline price.

## The checklist

1. **Same system, same terms.** Ask each installer to quote the same system size (kW) and the same panels, inverter and battery.
2. **Price per watt.** Divide each total price by the system size in watts. It is the quickest way to see which quote is really cheaper.
3. **What is included.** Permits, utility interconnection, monitoring, a battery if you want one, and any roof or electrical-panel work. Ask what is not included.
4. **Financing terms.** For a loan: the interest rate, the term and any dealer or origination fee built into the price. For a lease or PPA: the monthly payment, the yearly increase and what happens if you sell the home. See [solar lease vs buy](/guides/solar-lease-vs-buy).
5. **No federal tax credit.** The federal residential credit is not available for property placed in service after 2025, according to the IRS. Be wary of a quote that counts on it.
6. **Production estimate.** Ask how much electricity the system is expected to make each year and what shading and roof direction it assumes.
7. **Warranties.** Panels, inverter, battery and workmanship each have their own length. Ask who honors the workmanship warranty if the installer closes.
8. **License and insurance.** Check the installer's contractor license with your state (Florida: DBPR; California: CSLB) and ask for proof of insurance.
9. **Payment schedule.** Know the deposit and when each payment is due. Be cautious about large payments before work starts.

## Check the quote against your own numbers

Before you accept a quote, see what a typical system could cost and save for your utility: [FPL](/solar/florida/fpl), [Duke Energy Florida](/solar/florida/duke-energy-florida), [TECO](/solar/florida/teco), [PG&E](/solar/california/pge), [SCE](/solar/california/sce) or [SDG&E](/solar/california/sdge). Then run your own numbers in the [Florida calculator](/calculator/florida) or [California calculator](/calculator/california), or [request quotes](/quotes) from up to 3 installers.
