If you are in Scotland and hoping for a Home Energy Scotland grant or interest-free loan for solar panels, the current answer is no. Its grant and loan table lists only hybrid solar PV-T systems, with a £5,000 loan and no grant, and it does not list batteries. You can still finance solar with the UK-wide Warm Homes Loan, and installation is VAT-free until 31 March 2027.

## Key numbers

- **£0 grant, £0 loan** for standard solar PV panels or batteries in the Home Energy Scotland table (checked 22 Nov 2026).
- **£5,000 loan, no grant**: hybrid solar PV / water heating (PV-T); "only solar PV-T systems are eligible" (HES).
- **£7,500 grant + £7,500 loan**: heat pumps (HES).
- **£1,500**: rural and island uplift on heating and energy efficiency grants (HES).
- **£15,000**: Warm Homes Loan limit for solar, and another £15,000 for a battery, UK-wide (GOV.UK, Oct 2026).
- **862 to 922 kWh per kWp a year**: PVGIS yield for Glasgow, Edinburgh and Aberdeen.

## Does Home Energy Scotland fund solar panels?

Home Energy Scotland runs the Scottish Government's grant and loan scheme for homes. Its funding table, as published on 22 November 2026, includes:

| Measure | Loan | Grant |
|---|---|---|
| Air, ground or water source heat pumps | £7,500 | £7,500 |
| Heat network connection | £7,500 | £7,500 |
| Wood-fuelled (biomass) boilers and eligible stoves | £7,500 | £7,500 |
| Solar thermal | £5,000 | Not available |
| Hybrid solar PV / water heating (PV-T) | £5,000 | Not available |
| Wind turbine | £2,500 | Not available |
| Hydro turbine | £2,500 | Not available |

A footnote says only solar PV-T systems are eligible for the hybrid line. Standard rooftop PV panels and home batteries do not appear in the table. Some older articles and installer pages still mention Home Energy Scotland solar loans, so check the date on anything you read.

HES says funding is allocated first come, first served and is subject to availability.

## What help is there for solar in Scotland now?

- **Warm Homes Loan Scheme.** Launched on 27 October 2026 and available across the UK, including Scotland. The government factsheet lists loans of up to £15,000 for solar panels and up to £15,000 for a battery, from ten lenders at launch, some interest free. It works with MCS-certified installers.
- **0% VAT on installation.** Solar panels and batteries installed in homes are zero-rated until 31 March 2027, then 5% (HMRC). This applies in Scotland as elsewhere in the UK.
- **Smart Export Guarantee.** Scotland is in Great Britain, so SEG export tariffs are available. See [SEG rates compared](/uk/guides/best-seg-rates).
- **ECO4.** Ofgem says ECO4 runs until 31 December 2026. It is aimed at lower-income households and is delivered by energy suppliers. Do not plan around it for a 2027 install.

## Is solar worth it in Scotland without a grant?

Scotland gets less sun than southern England, but not by as much as people expect. PVGIS figures for an optimally tilted 1 kWp system:

| Place | kWh per kWp per year | Compared with London |
|---|---|---|
| Aberdeen | 922 | 90% |
| Edinburgh | 908 | 89% |
| Glasgow | 862 | 84% |
| London | 1,023 | 100% |

So a system in Edinburgh produces around 89% of what the same system would in London. Long summer days help. The bigger factors are still your roof direction, shading, how much power you use during the day and your export rate. Our UK-wide guide, [Are solar panels worth it in the UK?](/uk/guides/are-solar-panels-worth-it-uk), walks through those.

## How does that compare with England?

Scotland is not alone in having little direct solar funding. In England, the Warm Homes: Local Grant can include solar, but GOV.UK says the home must be privately owned with an EPC of D to G, and household income must usually be £36,000 a year or less. That scheme does not apply in Scotland. For most homeowners on either side of the border, the realistic support for solar in 2026 is the same: 0% VAT on installation until 31 March 2027, a Warm Homes Loan if you want to spread the cost, and an export tariff.

| Support | Scotland | England |
|---|---|---|
| Grant for standard solar PV | None in HES table | Warm Homes: Local Grant, low-income, EPC D–G |
| Warm Homes Loan (solar up to £15,000) | Yes | Yes |
| 0% VAT on installation to 31 Mar 2027 | Yes | Yes |
| Smart Export Guarantee | Yes | Yes |

## Should I consider PV-T instead to get the HES loan?

Only if it suits your home. PV-T panels make both electricity and heat, and they are often paired with heat pumps. The HES loan for PV-T is £5,000 with no grant. Choosing a technology to fit a loan, rather than your house, rarely pays. Ask an MCS-certified installer whether PV-T makes sense for your heating system.

## What about heat pumps with solar?

If you are replacing a gas or oil boiler, the HES heat pump grant of up to £7,500 (plus a £7,500 loan, and a £1,500 rural uplift where eligible) is the largest support on offer in Scotland. Solar panels can cut the running cost of a heat pump during the day. You would need to fund the solar part separately, for example through the Warm Homes Loan.

Scheme details were checked on 22 November 2026 and can change. Call Home Energy Scotland or check its website before you apply.
