Two state top-ups are clearly open in November 2026: Western Australia's Residential Battery Scheme (up to $1,300 for Synergy customers or $3,800 for Horizon Power customers) and the NSW virtual power plant incentive. The ACT offers a low-interest loan of up to $20,000 rather than a rebate. Queensland's Battery Booster, the NT's battery grants and Victoria's battery loans have all closed. Everyone with solar can still use the federal Cheaper Home Batteries rebate.

## Key numbers

- **WA:** **$130 per kWh** (Synergy) or **$380 per kWh** (Horizon Power), capped at 10 kWh, so **$1,300 or $3,800**. Minimum 5 kWh usable, and you must join a VPP. Confirmed open on 19 May 2026.
- **WA no-interest loans:** **$2,001 to $10,000** for households with income under **$210,000**.
- **NSW:** VPP incentive for batteries of **2 to 50 kWh**, scaling with capacity up to **28 kWh**. "No current closing date" (page updated 31 August 2026).
- **ACT:** loans of **$2,000 to $20,000 at 3%** over up to 10 years (October 2026).
- **VIC:** battery loans **closed**. The solar panel rebate (up to **$1,400**) is open, and moves into the VEU program from **1 July 2027**.
- **Closed:** QLD Battery Booster, NT Home and Business Battery Scheme (fully allocated, 10 June 2025), TAS Energy Saver Loan Scheme (1 September 2025, secondary source).

## Western Australia

The [scheme rules](https://www.wa.gov.au/organisation/energy-policy-wa/wa-residential-battery-scheme-eligibility-requirements) pay $380 per kWh of storage for Horizon Power customers up to $3,800, and $130 per kWh for Synergy customers up to $1,300. Rebate values are capped at 10 kWh, and the battery must have at least 5.0 kWh usable capacity. Rebates and loans "are only available to applicants that join a VPP product". Synergy customers choose Synergy's VPP or an approved alternative, and Horizon Power customers must use Horizon Power's.

No-interest loans run from $2,001 to $10,000 for households earning under $210,000. A [19 May 2026 media statement](https://www.wa.gov.au/government/media-statements/Cook%20Labor%20Government/More-than-20,000-WA-families-benefit-from-home-battery-savings-20260519) says the scheme "is still open for applications".

## New South Wales

NSW no longer gives households an upfront battery discount. Instead, [Energy NSW](https://www.energy.nsw.gov.au/households/grants-rebates/household-energy-saving-upgrades/connect-your-battery-virtual-power-plant) pays an incentive for connecting a battery to a virtual power plant. From 1 July 2026, batteries "up to 50 kWh are eligible", and the incentive depends on the capacity made available to the grid "up to 28 kWh". The page says batteries installed under the federal program up to 50 kWh "may be eligible", so it stacks. NSW does not publish a fixed dollar amount, because it varies with the VPP provider and certificate prices. Ask your VPP provider for the figure in writing.

## ACT

The ACT's [Sustainable Household Scheme](https://www.climatechoices.act.gov.au/policy-programs/sustainable-household-scheme) is a loan, not a rebate. It lends $2,000 to $20,000 at 3% over up to 10 years, and battery storage is on the eligible list. Households that applied before 1 July 2026 are limited to $15,000 over the life of the scheme. See our [ACT loan guide](/au/guides/act-sustainable-household-loan-guide).

## Victoria

[Solar Victoria](https://www.solar.vic.gov.au/solar-battery-rebate) "is no longer taking applications for interest-free loans for the installation of a solar battery system". The [solar panel rebate](https://www.solar.vic.gov.au/solar-panel-rebate) of up to $1,400, with an optional interest-free loan of the same amount, is still open. It is for owner-occupiers with combined taxable income under $150,000 and a property under $3 million. From 1 July 2027 that support moves into the Victorian Energy Upgrades program.

## South Australia

SolarQuotes reports a REPS incentive of up to $2,050 for connecting a battery to an approved VPP. It says funding for most households "ran out in May" 2026, with priority groups still eligible and a top-up expected in early 2027. We have not confirmed this on a government page, so treat it as unconfirmed.

## Closed schemes still being advertised

Old ads and articles still mention these, so check the date on anything you read:

- **Queensland Battery Booster.** The government page read "The Battery Booster program has now closed. Rebates are no longer available." The page has since been taken down.
- **NT Home and Business Battery Scheme.** The [NT government](https://dme.nt.gov.au/news/2025/home-and-business-battery-scheme-grants-fully-allocated) said on 10 June 2025 that it had reached its $6 million allocation and "is now closed to new grants".
- **Tasmania Energy Saver Loan Scheme.** It closed on 1 September 2025, according to [SolarQuotes](https://www.solarquotes.com.au/blog/tasmania-energy-saver-mb3265/).

If a seller quotes one of these as a current rebate, that is a red flag. See [battery rebate scams](/au/guides/battery-rebate-scams).

## The federal rebate still applies everywhere

The [Cheaper Home Batteries Program](https://cer.gov.au/batteries) applies in every state and territory, for batteries installed with solar. Its value steps down on 1 January 2027. See [what you lose by waiting](/au/guides/battery-rebate-changes-2027).
