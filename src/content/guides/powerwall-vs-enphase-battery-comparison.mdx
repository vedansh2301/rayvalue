Compare home batteries on five things: usable energy (kWh), continuous power (kW), how they connect to your solar, efficiency, and what the warranty actually guarantees. Tesla's Powerwall 3 and Enphase's IQ Battery 5P are built very differently, one large unit with a built-in solar inverter versus small modular units, so the brand matters less than whether the system you're quoted matches your outage needs and your bill.

## Key numbers (from each maker's 2025 North America datasheet)

- **Powerwall 3:** **13.5 kWh** nominal energy, up to **11.5 kW** AC continuous output, **20 kW** max solar input, **89%** solar-to-battery-to-home efficiency, **10-year** warranty.
- **IQ Battery 5P:** **5.0 kWh** usable, **3.84 kVA** continuous (7.68 kVA peak), **90%** AC round-trip efficiency, LFP chemistry, warranty to **15 years or 6,000 cycles** at more than 60% capacity.
- **Federal 25D credit:** **$0** for 2026+ installs (IRS).

## What to compare when choosing a home battery

### 1. Usable kWh, not just the headline

Usable capacity is the energy you can actually draw. Enphase lists the IQ Battery 5P's total and usable capacity at 5.0 kWh. Tesla lists Powerwall 3 as 13.5 kWh "nominal battery energy". Ask every installer for usable kWh, the number that decides how long the battery runs your home.

### 2. Continuous power (kW)

Energy (kWh) is how long it lasts. Power (kW) is how much it can run at once. Central AC, a well pump and an electric range at the same time need far more power than lights and a fridge. One Powerwall 3 is rated up to 11.5 kW continuous. One IQ Battery 5P is rated 3.84 kVA, so a whole-home Enphase backup usually means several units. Ask what the quoted system can run during an outage, and whether it's whole-home or a backed-up subpanel.

### 3. How it connects to your solar

Powerwall 3 has a solar inverter inside and accepts up to 20 kW of DC solar. Enphase's datasheet lists the IQ Battery 5P as compatible with Enphase IQ and M Series microinverters. If you already have solar, this matters most: a battery that doesn't suit your existing inverter can add equipment and cost. If you're on California's NEM 2, a change can also affect your grandfathered status; see [adding a battery without losing NEM 2](/guides/add-battery-to-nem-2-system).

### 4. Efficiency

Each round trip loses some energy. The two makers measure it differently (Tesla quotes solar-to-battery-to-home; Enphase quotes AC and DC round-trip), so don't compare 89% and 90% as if they were the same test. A few points matter less than size and price for most homes.

### 5. Warranty terms

Read the conditions, not just the years. Enphase's datasheet ties its warranty to keeping more than 60% capacity, for up to 15 years or 6,000 cycles. Tesla's datasheet lists 10 years; the capacity terms are in its separate warranty document. Ask who honors the warranty if the installer goes out of business.

## Which battery fits your goal?

| Your main goal | What to prioritize |
|---|---|
| Ride out outages (hurricanes, wildfire shutoffs) | Usable kWh for the hours you need, enough kW for your must-run loads, and the ability to recharge from solar during the outage |
| Cut bills in California under net billing | Enough kWh to cover your evening peak hours; compatibility with your rate plan |
| Add to an existing solar system | Compatibility with your current inverter; whether your utility treats it as a system change |
| Small budget | Modular systems let you start smaller; compare price per usable kWh |

## Price: compare per usable kWh

We don't publish brand prices because we have no primary source for 2026 installed prices of specific models. Our [home battery costs](/blog/home-battery-costs-and-backup) post covers what published cost studies say. When you get quotes, divide the installed price by usable kWh and compare like with like.

There's no federal tax credit for a battery installed in 2026 or later. In California, the CPUC's [SGIP](https://www.cpuc.ca.gov/industries-and-topics/electrical-energy/demand-side-management/self-generation-incentive-program/participating-in-self-generation-incentive-program-sgip) storage rebates prioritize high fire-threat areas, homes hit by two or more Public Safety Power Shutoffs, low-income and medically vulnerable customers. Check whether its budgets are open before you count on it.

Then test the numbers: the [California](/us/solar-payback/california) and [Florida](/us/solar-payback/florida) calculators show solar-plus-battery payback as a range.
