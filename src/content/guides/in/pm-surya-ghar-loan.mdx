You can borrow for rooftop solar under PM Surya Ghar **without collateral up to ₹2 lakh**, at a rate MNRE ties to the **repo rate plus 0.5%** for systems up to 3 kW. SBI, the largest lender, showed an **effective rate of 6.00%** for loans up to ₹2 lakh on its page dated 15 October 2026. When your subsidy is released, it goes **straight into the loan account**. A 3 kW system's ₹78,000 subsidy can therefore clear a large share of the loan within weeks of commissioning.

RayValue is an independent site, not a bank or a government portal.

## Key numbers

- **₹2 lakh**: collateral-free loan limit (PIB factsheet, 13 Mar 2025).
- **Repo + 0.5%**: MNRE's benchmark for standard loans for home systems up to 3 kW (MNRE guidelines).
- **6.75%**: rate quoted by PIB in March 2025, through 12 public sector banks.
- **6.00%**: SBI effective rate for loans up to ₹2 lakh, as of 15 Oct 2026. Above ₹2 lakh and up to ₹6 lakh, the rate was 8.15% (SBI).
- **10%**: minimum margin (your own contribution) of project cost (SBI).
- **120 months**: SBI's maximum tenure, including a 6-month moratorium. No prepayment penalty (SBI).
- **About 48%**: loan rejection rate reported in Delhi, cited in the Delhi Gazette of 17 Sep 2026.

## What is the PM Surya Ghar loan interest rate?

MNRE's operational guidelines say banks are expected to offer "standardized low interest loan products in the range of benchmark rates (Repo + 50 bps) for installation of residential RTS systems that are up to 3 kW in size." Similar loan products are meant to cover larger systems too.

Because the rate is linked to a benchmark, it changes with RBI policy:

| Source and date | Loan | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| PIB factsheet, 13 Mar 2025 | Up to ₹2 lakh, collateral-free | 6.75% |
| SBI page, FAQ dated 15 Dec 2025 | Up to ₹2 lakh | 5.75% |
| SBI page, rate box dated 15 Oct 2026 | Up to ₹2 lakh (EBLR − 2.15%) | 6.00% |
| SBI page, rate box dated 15 Oct 2026 | Above ₹2 lakh to ₹6 lakh (EBLR + 0%) | 8.15% |

SBI's own page shows both 5.75% and 6.00% in different sections. Confirm the rate in your sanction letter.

## Do I need collateral?

PIB says loans **up to ₹2 lakh** are collateral-free. SBI lists "Hypothecation of Assets" as security, meaning the solar system itself. SBI also lists these terms:

- **No minimum CIBIL score** for loans up to ₹2 lakh. Above ₹2 lakh, the minimum is 650, plus PAN, income proof and ITRs.
- **Age:** up to 65 years for an individual, or up to 75 with a co-borrower.
- **Processing fee:** nil (waived).
- **Insurance:** not mandatory for 1–3 kW. For larger systems, the asset must be insured at your cost.
- **Maximum system size:** 10 kW.

## How does the subsidy repay the loan?

This is the most useful feature of a portal-linked loan. MNRE's guidelines say:

> On redemption of the e-token ... the CFA will be transferred to the loan account of the consumer. If the CFA exceeds the outstanding loan amount ... the remainder [is] disbursed into the consumer's bank account.

So the sequence is:

1. The bank pays the vendor, and you pay your margin of at least 10%.
2. The system is installed, net-metered and inspected by the DISCOM.
3. You redeem the e-token on the portal. The subsidy of up to ₹78,000, or ₹85,800 in special category states, lands in your loan account.
4. Your outstanding loan drops by that amount. You repay the rest in EMIs.

The subsidy only reaches the loan account if the **loan details are on your portal profile**. They can be entered by you or come through bank integration. Check this before the inspection.

## How much would I borrow, and what would be left?

We don't have a sourced market price, so here are two reference costs for a 3 kW system:

| | MNRE benchmark | Delhi Gazette illustration |
|---|---|---|
| Project cost | ₹1,45,000 | ₹1,75,000 |
| Your 10% margin (SBI minimum) | ₹14,500 | ₹17,500 |
| Loan | ₹1,30,500 | ₹1,57,500 |
| Subsidy into loan account | ₹78,000 | ₹78,000 |
| Loan left after subsidy | ₹52,500 | ₹79,500 |

This is simple arithmetic on reference costs, not a quote. Interest also builds up on the full loan until the subsidy arrives. Delhi's Gazette says that usually takes one to two months after installation.

## How do I apply for a solar loan?

1. Register on [pmsuryaghar.gov.in](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/) and apply for rooftop solar.
2. Choose the **financing option on the portal**. MNRE says bank loan products are listed there and integrated through the Jan Samarth portal. You can also go to a bank directly.
3. Pick a registered vendor and get a quotation. The bank will want it.
4. Make sure your **loan account is recorded on your portal profile**, so the subsidy goes to it.
5. Install, complete net metering and inspection, then redeem the e-token.

## What should I watch out for?

- **Loan rejections are common.** Delhi's Gazette cites a reported rejection rate of about 48% in Delhi. That is one reason Delhi introduced vendor-paid models. See [Delhi rooftop solar subsidy 2026](/in/guides/delhi-solar-subsidy-2026).
- **Vendor finance is not the same as a scheme loan.** If a vendor arranges "easy EMI" at a higher rate, compare it with the bank's scheme rate.
- **No one can charge you to "approve" a loan or subsidy.** See [Rooftop solar scams and fake subsidy sites](/in/guides/rooftop-solar-scams-fake-subsidy-sites).
- **Mind the deadline.** The scheme runs till FY 2026-27, ending 31 March 2027. See [PM Surya Ghar last date](/in/guides/pm-surya-ghar-last-date).
