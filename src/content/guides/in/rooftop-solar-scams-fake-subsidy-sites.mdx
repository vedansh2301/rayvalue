The PM Surya Ghar subsidy has **one official application site, [pmsuryaghar.gov.in](https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/)**, and **no registration fee** in the official process. The subsidy is paid by the government **to you or your loan account**, not to the vendor, and only after your DISCOM inspects the system. Treat anyone who asks for a "registration" or "approval" fee, sends a link by WhatsApp, or promises a guaranteed subsidy as a likely scam.

## Key numbers and facts

- **1 official portal**: pmsuryaghar.gov.in. Vendors must be registered on it for the subsidy (MNRE guidelines).
- **₹0**: registration fee in the official nine-step process (PIB factsheet, 13 Mar 2025).
- **₹8,000**: the fake "registration fee" fraudsters demanded for MNRE's PM-KUSUM solar scheme, per a PIB Fact Check warning (8 Sep 2024).
- **15 days**: MNRE's target to release the subsidy after the DISCOM approves the system.
- **5 years**: free repairs and maintenance the registered vendor must provide (MNRE).

## What do solar subsidy scams look like?

These are the patterns to watch for. Each is checked against what the official rules say.

| Red flag | What the official rules say |
|---|---|
| A website asks you to "register" and pay a fee | The PIB process is: register on the National Portal with consumer number and mobile, then apply. No fee step. |
| You get an "approval letter" once you pay | PIB Fact Check called exactly this a fake when it was used for PM-KUSUM. Approval comes from your DISCOM on the portal. |
| A link arrives by SMS or WhatsApp | Type pmsuryaghar.gov.in yourself. Don't click forwarded links. |
| A vendor says "subsidy guaranteed" or "subsidy paid to us" | Under the standard scheme, CFA goes to the consumer's account (or loan account) only after DISCOM inspection. |
| A vendor wants you to sign over your portal login or OTP | You redeem the e-token yourself in your own profile. |
| "Imported panels, same subsidy" | Non-DCR modules "in any form" make the system ineligible (MNRE). |
| "Bigger system, bigger subsidy" | Subsidy stops growing at 3 kW: ₹78,000 maximum (₹85,800 in special category states). |
| "Last day tomorrow, pay now" | The scheme runs till FY 2026-27, ending 31 March 2027. |

## What has the government warned about?

The clearest official warning we could open is about MNRE's **PM-KUSUM** solar scheme, not PM Surya Ghar. On 8 September 2024 the PIB Fact Check unit warned that fraudsters were demanding **₹8,000 as a registration fee**. Victims then received a **fake approval letter**. MNRE urged people to "check the authenticity of the website before providing any personal information or depositing money".

We did not find a PIB or MNRE advisory specific to fake PM Surya Ghar sites (as of 22 Nov 2026). The same tactics carry over directly: a fake portal, a fee, and a fake letter. The defence is the same too.

## Who actually receives the subsidy?

This is the fact that defeats most scams. Under MNRE's guidelines for the standard PM Surya Ghar route:

1. You submit bank details and a cancelled cheque or passbook scan on **your own** portal profile.
2. The DISCOM physically inspects the system. "The CFA will be processed only after the DISCOM has physically verified the system."
3. You redeem the e-token in your profile, and the CFA is released **to your account**. If you have a scheme loan, it goes to your **loan account**.

The only official exceptions where subsidy goes to a vendor are state-run models such as Delhi's 2026 **ULA and DISCOM Trusteeship** models. There, vendors are selected by a government agency through bidding, not by a salesperson at your door. See [Delhi rooftop solar subsidy 2026](/in/guides/delhi-solar-subsidy-2026).

## How do I check a vendor?

- **Registration:** the vendor must be registered on the National Portal. MNRE says the scheme "shall be implemented only through the vendors registered on the National Portal". Find them through the portal's vendor list, not the vendor's own word.
- **DCR panels:** ask for the module make and DCR certificate in the quotation. Non-DCR panels make the system ineligible.
- **Written agreement:** MNRE provides a model vendor-consumer agreement. Insist on one with price, components, timelines and warranty.
- **Warranty and service:** five years of free repair and maintenance from commissioning, a five-year system warranty, and module performance warranty of at least 25 years.
- **Payment schedule:** avoid paying the full amount before installation. Tie the final payment to commissioning by the DISCOM.
- **Price:** MNRE does not set a price, and there is no government rate list. Its benchmark is ₹50,000 per kW for the first 2 kW. Get two or three quotes.

## Overstated savings claims

Not every problem is fraud. Some sales pitches promise "zero bill" or "free electricity" to every home. The scheme name says *Muft Bijli*, but:

- **Fixed charges stay** after solar in many states. Karnataka's 2026 regulations and MPERC's FY 2026-27 order both say so.
- **Exported units may earn little.** Karnataka pays ₹1.96–₹2.58 per unit for subsidised systems, and Tamil Nadu's net-metered surplus lapses on 31 March. See [Net metering by state](/in/guides/net-metering-by-state).
- **Low-slab homes save little per unit.** See [When rooftop solar doesn't pay](/in/guides/when-rooftop-solar-doesnt-pay).

## What do I do if I've been targeted?

1. Stop paying, and do not share OTPs or portal passwords.
2. Check your application status yourself on pmsuryaghar.gov.in. The portal has a grievance facility for genuine applications.
3. Report online financial fraud on the National Cybercrime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) or call the government's cyber fraud helpline, **1930**.
4. Inform your DISCOM if someone claimed to act for it.
